# Plan — T-210 A full first page with nothing of the title is read further

**Task:** A full first page of a name search with nothing of the title in it is read further (next pages, capped and
cached) before the title is called missing (B-027).
**Source:** BUGS.md B-027 — T-201 bug hunt pass 2: "Hunter X Hunter" → the archive's 50 newest "hunter" rows; the
2011 series (BG subs, 01–148, films, OVAs) at rows 488–711 of 796. Of 92 titles answered as missing in the replay,
19 have a full first page; Hunter x Hunter is the only real miss among them (the rest: games, music, other shows).
The archive answers at most 50 rows per call (`limit=200` → 50); `offset` pages it, and the live Oracle proxy passes
`offset` on (checked from the server 20.10, key kept on the server).

or series row carries the name; at most 1 000 rows; 4 pages at a time; the result remembered 6 h (an empty one
too); the rows found stored in the index for an archive outage. The replay stub must page too.
**Related bugs:** B-027
**Depends on:** T-209 (without it the Hunter x Hunter rows fail the show check), T-211 (without it the packs reach
later seasons)
**Security:** the proxy key stays in the request header as for every search; nothing new is logged.
