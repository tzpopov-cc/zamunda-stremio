# Plan — T-217 A season list is a pack of every season in it

**Task:** A release that lists its seasons ("Season 1,2,3", "Seasons 1 & 2", "Сезони 1 и 2") is a pack of each
listed season (B-035).
**Source:** BUGS.md B-035 — T-201 bug hunt pass 4: "NCIS Season 1,2,3 DVDRip-TVRip BGAudio DivX-bbb" (BG audio, on
the first page of "NCIS") is read as season 1 only; NCIS S2E3 was told „Няма сезон 2".
**Constraints:** "Season 2, Episode 5", "Season 1, 2019" are not lists; a single "Season N" and the ranges read as
before; "S01+S02+Movie" is read right already and stays.
**Related bugs:** B-035
**Depends on:** —
**Security:** not affected.
