**Task:** When the asked episode is in the archive only in releases without an infohash, the viewer is told so
(„Без връзка в архива", naming the season and episode), not that the season or episode is missing (B-036).
**Source:** BUGS.md B-036 — T-201 bug hunt pass 4: The Simpsons S9 („Няма сезон 9"; only "The Simpsons season 1-18

1", „Няма сезон 2"; only "Nip Tuck - Season 1", "- Season 2" without an infohash).
