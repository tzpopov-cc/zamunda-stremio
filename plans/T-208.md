# Plan — T-208 A film of the same name is not offered for a series

**Task:** A film of the same name is not offered for a series: a bare-named release or a "complete" pack that
looks like a film (filed as a film or Blu-ray disc, or years outside the show's run) is left out (B-024).
**Source:** BUGS.md B-024 — T-201 bug hunt pass 2: Carrie (2026) S1E1 → 21 rows of the 1976 and 2013 films;
Below (2026) S1E1 → "Below.2002"; Tom & Jerry (1940–1967) S1E1 → "Tom.and.Jerry.2021.MULTi.COMPLETE.BLURAY".
**Constraints:** a wrong stream is worse than an honest miss, but a real complete series must stay. Replay cache
(8 708 names): series are filed under „Филми" too — 96 arenabg, 65 zamunda, 57 zelka rows that name a season, and
complete packs that name none ("FRIENDS.Complete", "Avatar: The Last Airbender (Complete) (2005-2008)",
"Maya.The.Bee.1975 [KidsCollection]"). So the films category may only count where the release has nothing else
that says "series".
**Related bugs:** B-024 (B-025 recorded, not fixed here)
**Depends on:** T-207
**Security:** not affected.

## Steps

- [x] 1. The show's run from Cinemeta ("2012–2015", "2026–" → next year, "2026"). A release's years are read as one
      span; "outside the run" = the whole span more than a year away from it.
- [x] 2. Bare-show-name fallback (no season, no episode, no "complete" word): a release filed under films
      (documentaries aside) or Blu-ray, or with years outside the run, is not the show. Nothing left → the
      existing „Не е намерен" row.
- [x] 3. A "complete" pack that names no season ("COMPLETE", "COLLECTION", "BOX SET"…): left out only when filed
      under Blu-ray (a full disc — "COMPLETE.BLURAY") or its years are outside the run. The films category does not
      count here.
