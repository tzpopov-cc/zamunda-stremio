release that is this film), ask cinemeta-live once; if it names the title differently or dates it more than a year
apart, search again with its name and year, and use that answer when it finds the title at all (B-047, B-055).

host) — so nothing found today can be lost; one extra Cinemeta call per dead-end title a day (kept 24 h, a failure
not kept); the second search does not count as a second search on the dashboard; the GAP of the first one stays
logged.
